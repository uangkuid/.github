# Uangku

## About Uangku

Uangku is a company that develops financial tracking applications with a strong commitment to user privacy and security. Our core principles are built on **privacy-first** and **zero-knowledge** architecture, ensuring that your financial data remains completely private and secure.

### Key Features

- **Privacy First**: Your financial data is yours alone. We design our applications with privacy as the foundation, not as an afterthought.
- **Zero Knowledge**: We implement zero-knowledge architecture, meaning we have no access to your sensitive financial information. Your data is encrypted end-to-end, and only you hold the keys.
- **Financial Tracking**: Comprehensive tools to help you manage and track your personal finances effectively.

### Our Commitment

At Uangku, we believe that financial privacy is a fundamental right. Our zero-knowledge approach ensures that even we, as the service provider, cannot access your financial records. This gives you complete control and peace of mind when managing your finances.
